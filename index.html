A highway resort with two markets

  • Weekdays: corporate stays, conferences and events from the Bidadi industrial area
  • Weekends: Bangalore families driving out on SH17 towards Mysuru, 32 km from the city
  • Monsoon (May to October): indoor gaming zone and theatres serve team-building groups
  • Setting: next to Eagleton Golf Club, in an area drawing residential and industrial development

The claim that no international upscale hotel operates within 25 km still needs to be confirmed.

What has to be true

  • ADR of about ₹12,000. Check against comparable Marriott Collection hotels near Bangalore. At ₹10,500 the return falls to about 12%.
  • Project cost of ₹250 Cr. This needs value engineering and firm contractor quotes. At ₹300 Cr the return is about 12%.
  • Extra revenue of 50% over rooms. At 40%, Year 1 debt cover falls below 1.0 and the return is about 15%.
  • Site and weather risks. Zoning of corporate and water-park guests on a compact site, long monsoons, and interest rates.
